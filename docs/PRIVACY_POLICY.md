# Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date: To be added before publishing

PetCare respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the PetCare app collects, how it is used, and what choices users have.

This is a draft. Review it with a qualified legal adviser before publishing the app.

## Information We Collect

PetCare may collect information that users provide directly in the app, including:

- Account name and email address.
- Profile details.
- Pet names, photos, species, breed, age, and health details.
- Vaccination records.
- Appointment records.
- Medication records.
- Weight records.
- Medical documents and files uploaded by the user.
- Emergency profile information selected by the user.
- Veterinarian or clinic details entered by the user.

The app may also collect technical information required to provide app functionality:

- Authentication identifiers.
- Device notification tokens.
- Approximate or precise location when users choose to find nearby clinics.
- Basic diagnostic or service logs from Firebase services.

## How We Use Information

PetCare uses information to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Store and display pet care records.
- Schedule appointments, vaccinations, medications, and reminders.
- Send app notifications.
- Upload and display pet photos and medical documents.
- Show nearby clinics when location access is granted.
- Support emergency profile sharing.
- Allow owners to share selected pet information with veterinarians.
- Improve reliability, security, and app functionality.

## Firebase And Third-Party Services

PetCare uses Firebase services, which may process data on behalf of the app:

- Firebase Authentication.
- Cloud Firestore.
- Firebase Storage.
- Firebase Cloud Messaging.
- Firebase Cloud Functions.
- Firebase Hosting.

The app may also use Google Sign-In, map/location services, and network APIs required for clinic lookup and app functionality.

## Sharing Of Information

PetCare does not sell user information.

Information may be shared only in these cases:

- With veterinarians or doctors when a pet owner chooses to share pet access.
- Through an emergency public profile when the user enables or shares that profile.
- With Firebase and service providers required to operate the app.
- When required by law, security, or abuse prevention.

## Emergency Public Profiles

PetCare may allow users to create public emergency profiles for pets. Anyone with the emergency profile link or QR code may view the information included in that public profile. Users should only include information they are comfortable making available for emergency care purposes.

## Location Data

PetCare requests location access only for features such as finding nearby veterinary clinics or placing a clinic at the current location. Users can deny or disable location permission in device settings.

## Notifications

PetCare may request notification permission to send reminders for care tasks such as appointments and vaccinations. Users can disable notifications in device settings.

## Data Security

PetCare uses Firebase services and encrypted network connections to help protect data in transit. No system is completely secure, so users should protect their account credentials and device access.

## Account And Data Deletion

Users can request account and associated data deletion through the app if the feature is available, or by contacting support.

## Children's Privacy

PetCare is not intended for children without appropriate parent or guardian involvement. If the app is offered to children or families, the developer must comply with all applicable child privacy and Google Play Families policies.

## Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated policy should include a new effective date.
